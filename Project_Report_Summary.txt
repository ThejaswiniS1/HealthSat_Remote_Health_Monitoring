HealthSat – Project Report Summary

1. Introduction
HealthSat is an IoT and satellite-based communication project designed to bring healthcare connectivity to rural communities. It aims to monitor vital health parameters remotely and alert hospitals during medical emergencies.

2. Problem Statement
Rural areas often lack proper medical infrastructure and internet coverage, leading to delayed healthcare services. There is a need for a low-cost, low-power health monitoring solution that can operate beyond cellular networks.

3. Proposed System
The proposed system uses an ESP32 microcontroller with a MAX30105 sensor to collect heart rate and oxygen saturation data. Data is transmitted through LoRa (SX1276) modules to a receiver unit connected to a Streamlit dashboard. The dashboard visualizes patient vitals and triggers alerts when abnormal readings are detected.

4. Advantages
- Real-time continuous health monitoring  
- Reliable communication without internet dependency  
- Low power consumption  
- Quick emergency response through hospital alerts  
- Scalable deployment across remote villages

5. Results and Discussion
The prototype successfully transmitted heart rate and IR sensor readings via LoRa up to several hundred meters. The Streamlit dashboard visualized the data effectively, demonstrating system feasibility for real-world use.

6. Conclusion
HealthSat provides a reliable, cost-effective, and scalable solution for remote health monitoring. It can bridge the healthcare access gap in underserved regions and can be expanded to include additional vital sensors in future work.

7. Future Enhancements
Integration of temperature and ECG sensors, cloud-based health analytics, and AI-driven early disease detection modules.
