HealthSat – A Remote Health Monitoring Communication Satellite

The HealthSat project proposes an IoT-based remote health monitoring system designed to provide continuous healthcare access in rural and isolated regions with limited communication infrastructure. The system utilizes an ESP32 microcontroller integrated with a MAX30105 pulse oximeter sensor to measure vital parameters such as heart rate and SpO₂ levels. These readings are transmitted using a LoRa (SX1276) communication module, which ensures long-range, low-power data transfer without relying on conventional cellular or internet connectivity. A Streamlit-based hospital dashboard receives and visualizes the data in real-time, allowing healthcare professionals to monitor patient conditions continuously and respond quickly in emergencies.

The proposed system enhances healthcare accessibility by offering reliable data transmission and continuous monitoring even in areas with poor or no network coverage. It is highly power-efficient, cost-effective, and scalable for multi-village deployment. HealthSat demonstrates how the integration of IoT and LoRa technology can transform rural healthcare delivery, providing a foundation for future satellite-based medical communication networks.
